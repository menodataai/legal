Getting a good recording

Almost every disappointing result traces back to the filming rather than the analysis. Three things matter most:

  • Keep the phone still. A tripod, a fence post, or a bag will do. The app measures distances in the frame, so a moving camera moves the measurements.
  • Get the whole serve in frame — including the top of the toss. If the ball leaves the top of the picture, the app cannot see where it peaked and will decline to report the reach.
  • Leave the sound on. The sound of the racquet striking the ball is how the app finds the moment of contact. Recording silently still works, but it works less well.

Side-on or behind?

The app asks which way the camera was pointing, and the answer changes the analysis — so it is worth getting right. Read the court, not the player:

  • Behind — the net runs across the picture, and the baseline is a horizontal line at the player's feet.
  • Side-on — the baseline recedes away from you as a near-vertical line the player straddles, there is usually no net in shot, and after contact the player travels across the frame rather than towards you.

Why does a tile show “—”?

Because the app could not measure that value from the footage it was given, and declined to guess.

This is deliberate. A missing number is honest; an invented one quietly corrupts every comparison you make afterwards. Common causes are the toss leaving the top of the frame, the ball being hidden behind the player, or the contact sound being lost in wind or a noisy hall.

If a tile is always empty across many serves, that is worth reporting — it usually means something about your setup that we can fix.

Your videos and your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no upload and no server — the app makes no network requests at all. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Getting your data out

Your recordings and analysis results are visible in the Files app under ServeCoach, and in Finder when the iPhone is connected to a computer. Copy or delete them there.

Deleting everything

Delete a session in the app to remove its data, or delete the app to remove all of it at once. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because nothing was sent anywhere else.

Filming other people's children

Many people using this app are coaches, filming somebody else's child. The app keeps that footage private — it never leaves the phone. Getting the parent's or guardian's permission before recording is still yours to do.

Requirements

  • iPhone, iOS 17 or later. iPhone only for now — there is no iPad version.
  • Analysis runs on the device and uses its Neural Engine; a longer clip takes longer on an older phone.
