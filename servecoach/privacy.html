ServeCoach — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 September 2026  ·  App: ServeCoach for iPhone  ·  Published by: Meno Data AB, Sweden

ServeCoach does not collect anything. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The app's own code makes no network requests — there is no server for your videos to go to, because we did not build one. Buying or restoring ServeCoach Pro is handled by Apple's App Store on your device; we receive nothing from it but Apple's anonymised sales totals. Everything the app records, measures and remembers stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

That includes video of the person you are filming. We never receive it, and we cannot.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no category of personal data that ServeCoach transmits to us or to anyone else.

This is a property of how the app is built rather than a promise about how we behave: the app contains no networking code and no third-party software development kits. The one thing that talks to a server is Apple's own StoreKit, when you buy or restore ServeCoach Pro — that is Apple's transaction with you, under Apple's privacy policy, and the app never sees your payment details. It is the same claim as the “Data Not Collected” label on the App Store product page.

What stays on your iPhone

All of this is stored inside the app's own container on your device:

  • Videos you record in the app, or import from your photo library.
  • Analysis results — the timing of each phase of a serve, the measured heights and distances, and the per-frame body-joint coordinates the analysis is derived from.
  • Player profiles you type in — a name or initials, height, handedness.
  • Preferences, such as the camera view and venue you last chose.

Deleting a session in the app deletes its stored data. Deleting the app removes all of it.

The app also makes this data visible to you through the Files app, and through Finder when your iPhone is connected to a computer. That is deliberate: it is your data, and you should be able to take it out or delete it without asking us.

Permissions, and why each is asked

  • Camera — to record a serve. Only while the recording screen is open.
  • Microphone — the sound of the racquet striking the ball is how the app finds the moment of contact. Without audio the app still works, but finding serves is slower and less reliable.
  • Photo library (add only) — to save a copy of a serve you recorded into your own Photos, if you choose to. The app never asks for permission to read your library: importing uses the system photo picker, which hands the app only the single video you select.

You can withdraw any of these in iOS Settings at any time.

The only two ways anything leaves your device

Both require you to act, and neither happens on its own:

  • The share button. If you export a session report, iOS hands that file to whichever app or person you pick. Where it goes after that is determined by your choice, not by ServeCoach.
  • Saving to Photos. If you tap the option to keep a copy of a recording, the video is copied into your own photo library, which may sync to iCloud depending on your iCloud settings.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymised crash reports about ServeCoach. These come from Apple, not from the app. They describe where the software failed; they contain no video, no images and no analysis results. If that setting is off, we receive nothing.

While ServeCoach is in TestFlight

If you are testing a pre-release version through TestFlight, Apple tells us the email address you were invited with, and shows us any feedback or screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight. That is Apple's system, described in Apple's own privacy policy. It still does not give us access to your videos or your analysis results.

Children

ServeCoach exists to analyse a serve, and the person serving is very often a child. This matters more than anything else on this page, so we will be precise:

  • Video of the person being filmed, and the body-joint data derived from it, never leave the device. We have no access to them at any point.
  • The app is designed for the adult holding the phone — a parent or a coach — not for a child to use on their own. It has no social features, no messaging, no user-generated content shared with anyone, and no links out of the app except to iOS Settings.
  • Consent is yours to obtain. If you film someone else's child, getting the permission of their parent or guardian is your responsibility. The app makes the recording safe to keep; it cannot make the decision to record.

Your control over your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, share, sell or delete on your behalf. Your data is on your device, and you can view, export or delete it there — through the app, through the Files app, or by deleting the app.

If a future version of ServeCoach ever collects anything, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date, and where they affect what the app does, in the release notes for the version that introduces them.
